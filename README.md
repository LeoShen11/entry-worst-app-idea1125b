# BlinkVault — The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched

A one-sentence pitch for a brilliantly useless app, written by `main.py`.

Running `python3 main.py` writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`.
The pitch is a single sentence of at most 240 characters: a subscription
app that photographs every blink and then sells the blurry eyelid album
back to its owner, with paid unlocks for the supposedly emotional blinks.
